# Privacy Policy — YouTube Uploader

Last updated: 2026-09-01

YouTube Uploader is a private, owner-operated tool used to publish approved
videos to the owner's YouTube channel. It is not made available to the general
public.

## Google user data accessed

After the owner grants permission through Google OAuth, the tool may use the
YouTube Data API to:

- upload and schedule videos;
- read and update video titles, descriptions, tags, visibility, and thumbnails;
- add uploaded videos to the owner's configured playlist;
- read and manage comments when the owner explicitly runs that function; and
- read channel and processing status needed to verify the correct upload.

The OAuth scopes requested by the current desktop uploader are
`youtube.upload` and `youtube.force-ssl`.

## Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's local
computer. They are excluded from this Git repository. The tool does not sell,
rent, share, or use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose
unrelated to the owner's requested YouTube publishing workflow.

Episode metadata and non-secret verification records may be stored in this
repository. OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets, and private
account data must never be committed.

## Retention and deletion

Local OAuth credentials remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes
them or revokes the app's access in the Google Account connections page. Video
and channel data remains subject to the owner's YouTube settings and Google's
retention policies.

## Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services
User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
