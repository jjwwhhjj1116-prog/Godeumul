# YouTube Uploader

YouTube Uploader is a private, owner-operated automation tool for the YouTube
channel **고대유물의 비밀**. It uploads approved local video masters and
thumbnails, applies the episode title and description, schedules publication,
and verifies the resulting YouTube video state.

The tool is not offered as a public service. It is used only by the project
owner and accounts personally authorized by the owner.
