## 유물 형태 문장 — ★ 이 문장을 매 컷 복사

### ① 봉토 (외경) — EP01 최대 실패 지점

**실제**: 흙을 다져 쌓은 **사각 계단식 절두 피라미드**(覆斗形).
밑변 약 350m 정사각형, 현재 높이 약 50m(원래 약 76m로 추정), 3단 계단.
**둥근 언덕이 아니다.**

```
a large square stepped truncated-pyramid earthen mound of rammed loess,
three broad stepped tiers rising to a flat rectangular top, square base
roughly 350 metres per side and about 50 metres tall, straight ridge
lines and clearly squared corners, grass and low scrub growing over the
tiers — a squared artificial mound, NOT a round natural hill
```

### ② 지궁 (지하 궁전) — 미발굴

```
a rectangular underground palace chamber complex on a north-south axis,
rammed-earth and stone retaining walls, timber roof beams over the
central chamber, surrounded by a rectangular walled enclosure
```

> ★ 미발굴이므로 화면 우상단에 `RECONSTRUCTION` 라벨을 단다
> (`00.팩트체크.md` 지궁 미발굴 고지).

### ③ 청동 노기 (弩機) — 석궁 방아쇠. 병마용갱 출토 실물 있음

```
a Qin dynasty bronze crossbow trigger mechanism (nuji), a compact cast
bronze assembly of exactly three moving parts inside a bronze housing:
the vertical string catch (wang) that holds the drawn bowstring, the
long downward trigger lever (xuandao), and the short pivot link
connecting them, joined by two bronze pivot pins, surfaces a deep green
verdigris over warm bronze, precise machined-looking fit
```

### ④ 노(弩) 본체

```
a Qin dynasty crossbow: a straight lacquered wooden stock about one
metre long, a composite bow of wood and horn lashed across the front
with cord, the bronze trigger housing inlaid flush into the rear of the
stock, black lacquer finish with bronze fittings
```

### ⑤ 병마용 (참고 — 본편에 직접 안 나옴)

```
life-size terracotta warrior figures in Qin lamellar armour over
knee-length robes, hair in a side topknot, individually distinct faces,
unglazed earthenware with faint traces of pigment
```
