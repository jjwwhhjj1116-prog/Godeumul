# EP05 로제타석 썸네일

## 확정 문구

- 상단 키커: `고대 이집트를 깨운 세 문자`
- 큰 금색 제목: `로제타석`

## 생성 프롬프트

```text
Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: 9:16 YouTube Shorts thumbnail background for 고대유물의 비밀
Primary request: the real Rosetta Stone as the single unmistakable foreground artifact.
Scene/backdrop: a dark Ptolemaic Egyptian temple and archaeological setting near the Nile, restrained wall reliefs and distant warm torchlight, two stops darker and softer than the artifact.
Subject: a large grey-and-pink granodiorite fragment with an irregular broken upper outline and three visibly different inscription-density zones: hieroglyphic at top, Demotic in the middle, Ancient Greek at bottom.
Style/medium: premium photorealistic museum-display historical reconstruction, tactile stone microtexture and crisp incisions.
Composition/framing: exact 9:16; locked camera, eye level, 50–85mm product lens; upper 24 percent clean dark negative space; artifact centered and fully recognizable below the title zone.
Lighting/mood: strong warm pin spotlight with subtle cool rim light, deep black background and high foreground/background separation.
Constraints: no title text, no logo, no watermark, no UI, no extra hero artifact; no cuneiform tablet, no fantasy runes, no invented complete stela top.
```

배경은 이미지 생성 도구로 만들고, 한글은 `tools/thumbnail_build.py`로 합성했다.
