# EP06 안티키테라 기계 썸네일

## 확정 문구

- 상단 키커: `바닷속에서 나온 2천 년 전 컴퓨터`
- 큰 금색 제목: `안티키테라 기계`

## 배경 제작 기준

```text
Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: 9:16 YouTube Shorts thumbnail background for 고대유물의 비밀
Primary request: the surviving corroded bronze Antikythera Mechanism fragments as the single unmistakable foreground artifact.
Scene/backdrop: dark archaeological museum display with a restrained Aegean shipwreck atmosphere, two stops darker and softer than the artifact.
Subject: Fragment A dominates the frame, with two smaller real-style fragments below; thick green patina, limestone accretion, exposed toothed bronze wheels and readable mechanical layering.
Style/medium: premium photorealistic archaeological conservation portrait, tactile corrosion microtexture, crisp edges and no fantasy steampunk completion.
Composition/framing: exact 9:16; locked camera, 70–100mm museum product lens; upper 23 percent clean dark negative space; artifact centered, large and sharply separated from the background.
Lighting/mood: strong warm pin spotlight from upper right, restrained cool rim light, deep black background, high color and luminance separation.
Constraints: no title text, no logo, no watermark, no sparkle, no UI, no modern clock face, no invented intact case, no extra hero artifact.
```

배경 생성 뒤 `tools/thumbnail_build.py`가 검증된 Gmarket Sans Bold 골격과 채널 금박·돌출·그림자 스타일로 한글을 합성했다. 최종 파일은 `썸네일.jpg`이며 1080×1920, 2MB 미만이다.
